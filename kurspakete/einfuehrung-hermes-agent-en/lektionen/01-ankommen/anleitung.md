# Getting started

Welcome! In this course package you work with **Hermes**, an AI agent – in your own **Agent PC**.

## How this guide works

- This window stays next to your Agent PC. **"Continue"** takes you to the next lesson, **"Back"** to the previous
  one.
- Some lessons have a **check task**: click **"Check"** when you're done. You only see "done" or "not yet" with a
  tip.
- Some lessons end with a short **quiz**. The result stays on your computer.
- If something went wrong, **"↺ Reset"** resets your Agent PC to the state at the beginning of the lesson.

## In a course

- If you want to say something: **✋ Raise hand**.
- If you're stuck: **🆘 Request help**. The trainer only sees your Agent PC if you agree – never your own screen.
- Microphone and camera are off at the start. Turn them on yourself if you like.

## On your own (self-study mode)

You don't need a trainer: work through the lessons at your own pace. The times are only a guide.

::: tipp
**You're allowed to make mistakes in the Agent PC.** It's a separate computer in a window. Only it can break – not
your own computer.
:::
