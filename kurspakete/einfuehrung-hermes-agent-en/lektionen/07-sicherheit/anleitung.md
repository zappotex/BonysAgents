# Safety

## What I don't give an AI

- no **passwords**, **account details** or **health data**,
- no data about **other people**,
- my **API key** or my sign-in only in `hermes setup` – never in a chat and never while I'm sharing my screen.

## Checking answers

Especially **numbers, dates, legal and health matters**. An AI always sounds sure – even when it's wrong.

## Costs

Free models have limits. Paid models charge per text – set a **limit** in your account with the provider.

## Hidden instructions

A web page can contain text you don't see – for example *"Ignore all previous instructions and …"*. If Hermes reads
the page, it might take that as an order (**prompt injection**). So: no important data in the Agent PC, and ask back
when steps look unusual.

::: datenschutz
The Agent PC protects your computer – but **whatever is in the Agent PC, the AI can see and change**.
:::
