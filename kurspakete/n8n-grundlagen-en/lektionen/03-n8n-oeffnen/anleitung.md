# Opening n8n

## 1. Start n8n

Double-click **"n8n"** on your Agent PC's desktop. n8n opens in Brave at `http://localhost:5678`.

::: hinweis
After the Agent PC starts, n8n needs about **a minute**. If no page appears, wait a moment and reload (F5).
:::

## 2. Create an account

The first time you open it, n8n shows **"Set up owner account"**: e-mail address, first and last name, password. This
account **only applies to your n8n** in this Agent PC – it isn't sent anywhere. Remember the password.

Then n8n asks a few things about you and your work – you can leave them empty and go on with **"Continue"**.

## 3. Look around

You see your workflows (none yet), plus the credentials (**Credentials**) and the logs of the runs
(**Executions**).

::: datenschutz
n8n only runs in your Agent PC. It can't be reached from your home network or the Bony Network.
:::

Done? Click **"Check"**.
