# What is a workflow?

A **workflow** is a process that always runs the same way – just like in everyday life: *When mail arrives, I put it
in the tray.* n8n puts such processes together with a few clicks.

## The building blocks

| Building block | Meaning | Everyday example |
|---|---|---|
| **Trigger** | when the workflow starts | "When mail arrives …" |
| **Node** | a single work step | "… read the sender …", "… put it in the tray" |
| **Connection** | in which order the data moves on | the way from the mailbox to the tray |
| **Execution** | a single run, with a log | "today at 10 a letter arrived" |

## What we build today

**RSS Feed Trigger** (new item in the feed) → **Telegram** (send it to my phone).

An **RSS feed** is a list of a website's latest articles that programs can read. Many news sites offer one.

## n8n or Hermes?

**n8n** does exactly the same thing every time – reliable and predictable. **Hermes** understands language and
decides by itself. The two can be combined later – today it's just n8n.
