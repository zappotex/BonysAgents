- Draw (or show) a picture: computer → internet provider → website; then the tunnel to the VPN server. Pick up the
  question from lesson 1: on café Wi-Fi without a VPN, the operator sees where you go.
- The right-hand column is what matters: **"not anonymous"**. Anyone who really needs to be anonymous needs Tor – and
  that has limits too. Don't make promises a VPN doesn't keep.
- Choosing a provider: where the company is based, no logs ("no-logs", ideally independently audited), payment, a
  free plan with limits (speed, server choice, one device). Proton VPN is just an example today, not an ad.
- If someone asks "And streaming from abroad?": often works, but isn't the topic and sometimes breaks the services'
  terms.
