# Getting started

Welcome! Today you set up a **VPN** – with **Bony's VPN** in your Agent PC. By the end you're browsing through an
encrypted tunnel, the **kill switch** is watching, and you know what a VPN protects – and what it doesn't.

## How the course works

1. What is a VPN? Benefits and limits
2. Getting the configuration – from Proton VPN (free) or from the FRITZ!Box
3. Import, connect, check
4. Kill switch and connecting automatically
5. Exercise: browsing more privately
6. Cleaning up and wrap-up

## Before we start

- Start your Agent PC (main window → choose the Agent PC → **"Start"**).
- The **"Bony's VPN"** icon is on the desktop.
- Have an **e-mail address** ready (for a free account at Proton VPN) – or access to your **FRITZ!Box**.

::: hinweis
If you're stuck: **🆘 Request help**. Today the VPN only applies to your Agent PC – your own computer stays as it is.
:::
