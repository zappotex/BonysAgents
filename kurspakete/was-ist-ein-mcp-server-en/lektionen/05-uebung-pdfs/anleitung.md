# Exercise: Hermes works with PDFs

In `~/Agent-Files/pdf` there are fresh sample PDFs: `invoice-march.pdf`, `invoice-april.pdf` and `minutes.pdf`.
Tell Hermes to use **PdfCraft** – otherwise it might look for a detour.

## 1. Summarize

*"Use PdfCraft: summarize minutes.pdf. What was resolved?"*

Check **yourself**: open `minutes.pdf` in the file manager (folder `Agent-Files` → `pdf`, double-click). Are the
three resolutions right?

## 2. Rotate a page

*"Use PdfCraft: page 2 of minutes.pdf is sideways. Rotate it upright and save the result as minutes-upright.pdf."*

Open `minutes-upright.pdf` – is page 2 the right way up now?

## 3. Merge

*"Use PdfCraft: merge invoice-march.pdf and invoice-april.pdf into invoices-total.pdf."*

Open `invoices-total.pdf` – two pages, March first?

Then click **"Check"**.

::: tipp
**For fast learners:** *"How much are both invoices together?"* – and calculate it yourself.
:::

::: warnung
PdfCraft is still **alpha**. With your own important PDFs, always work on a **copy**.
:::
